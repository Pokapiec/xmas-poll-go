## Scope

All pages of Xmas Gift Poll (home/create, organizer, person wishlist, 404). Visitor mode: Operate. Phone-first, bilingual PL (default) / EN.

## Audience and task

Organizer: add people, shuffle pairs, send each person their link. Participant: write at least 3 wishes, then see who they buy for and that person's wishes. Older relatives included.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The draw is an advent-calendar door: it stays shut until you have written three wishes, then it opens on the person you buy for. Refuses the generic white SaaS card stack and the snowflake/clip-art Christmas page.

OWN-WORLD: Printed advent calendar. Snow ground (#f7f6f2), pine header band (#1e4d3a), calendar red (#9e1b32) for the door and primary actions, gold-foil numerals (#d9a441) on red tiles, ink #1c1c1c. Bricolage Grotesque headings, system sans body. Lucide line icons. Dark mode is the same calendar at night: pine-black ground, same red and gold.

STORY: Participant sees greeting, the door (countdown or reveal), their list and the add form. Organizer sees people as numbered door tiles with wish pips and link buttons, then the draw.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Pine header with brand and PL/EN switch, h1 greeting, party subtitle under it; red door card with 72px gold numeral tile and the remaining-wishes count; white list card below with the add form and a full-width red button.

FORM: Advent Calendar Doors, position 1 on my grounded list (picked by the user over assigned candidate 3), seed key 9bf5bc66. Signature motion: the door swings open once, when the third wish unlocks the draw.
